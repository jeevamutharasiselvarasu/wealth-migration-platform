Infer rules from business-rule text

Paste business rules, one per line or Markdown bullet. Recognised sentences become draft rules with your wording kept as the source; nothing is used until a Migration Analyst or Platform Admin approves it. Supported: map A to B / A -> B, dates are MM/DD/YYYY, strip $ and , with 2 decimals, concatenate F1 and F2 with a space, split on "-" and take the second part, default to "X" if blank, replace "X" with "Y". Anything else is reported, never guessed.

Register business rules as a knowledge asset

Write only rule lines (and "#" comment lines) in the file. A plain prose line is read as a rule and reported as not recognised. Example file: demo_data/knowledge/corecap_business_rules.md.
