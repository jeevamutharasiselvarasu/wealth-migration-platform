Entity resolution

When several source rows are the same real-world record (the same client in two rows), the approved match rules turn them into one canonical record. Rows linked by any approved rule are one record. The most complete row survives and its blank fields are filled from the others. Every source key is kept with its surviving key, and references from other entities (an account role's client) point at the survivor. Masked or missing values (such as [REDACTED]) never match.
